Working for animals, together.

The Animal Welfare Hub brings together people in and around Cologne who work on food system change: making plant-based food the norm and moving away from animal products. We share a room and each work on our own projects.

Join Who is it for?

For people who are already serious about it

Whether you run your own project, work for an NGO or do research: the hub is a place to work alongside people who care about the same thing.

  • You take this work seriously, full-time or part-time.
  • You help make plant-based food the norm.
  • You can get to Cologne.

What a hub day is like

We work in the same room, each on our own projects. In between, we share what we have learned, brainstorm together when someone is stuck and open up our networks to each other.

Everything beyond that we shape together as the group finds its rhythm.

Where things stand

It started with two people working together once a week. Now it is becoming a pilot.

Group
Five to eight people to start
Days
One or two a week to start, then we see how it grows
Place
Being sorted out, ideally near the University of Cologne

The hub is an independent project. It was started by Florian Burger, who is doing a master’s in mathematics at the University of Cologne and campaigns with Plant-based Universities for the Kölner Studierendenwerk to switch its canteens to fully plant-based food.
