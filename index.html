Working for animals, but not alone.

The Animal Welfare Hub brings together people in and around Cologne who work full-time on animal welfare or firmly plan to. On two fixed days a week, we work in the same room, each on our own projects.

Sign up Who is it for?

For people who are already serious about it

If you have an NGO job, you have colleagues and a structure. If you are building your own project, you are often alone at your desk. The hub is for the second group.

  • You work full-time on animal welfare or firmly plan to, alone or with two or three others.
  • You don’t have to found anything. Researchers and people writing papers belong here just as much.
  • Animal welfare is the core. Food system change, climate and food, or alternative proteins fit too.
  • To start with, the hub is for anyone who can commute to Cologne.

What a hub day looks like

  1. Morning

    Short check-in

    Everyone says what they want to get done today.

  2. Lunch

    Eating together

    Time to hear about each other’s work.

  3. End of the day

    Short review

    What worked, where you are stuck, what comes next.

Once a month, someone presents their project and gets feedback from the group.

Where things stand

It started with two people working together once a week. Now it is becoming a pilot.

Group
Five to eight people to start
Days
Two fixed days a week
Place
Being sorted out, ideally near the University of Cologne

The hub is an independent project. It was started by Florian Burger, who is doing a master’s in mathematics at the University of Cologne and campaigns with Plant-based Universities for the Kölner Studierendenwerk to switch its canteens to fully plant-based food.
